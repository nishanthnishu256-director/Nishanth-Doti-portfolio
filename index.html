01 / The filmmaker

Stories first.
Always.

A filmmaker driven by emotion, visual storytelling and the belief that every story deserves its own voice.

I am Nishanth Doti — a film director, writer and editor based in Hyderabad, India.

My work brings together storytelling, performance and visual expression. I am interested in stories that stay with audiences beyond the final frame.

SAHAA — Life of Sanju is my debut feature film, marking an important step in my filmmaking journey.
